The DragSelectingTool has been replaced by the LassoSelectingTool. Mouse-down, wait 175 milliseconds, and drag the mouse around all nodes that you want to select. To be selected, a Node's Node.selectionObject must be completely within the temporarily-drawn magenta polygon. You can use the Shift and Ctrl modifiers to affect how the selection is affected.

The tool is defined in its own file, as LassoSelectingTool.js.
